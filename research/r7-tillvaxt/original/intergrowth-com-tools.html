The following INTERGROWTH-21st resources are available for clinicians and researchers. The links below will allow you to access and download all publications, tables, charts, calculators, apps and training resources. A number of these have been translated into several languages and are available here.

Practitioners can download and use these charts freely. To connect with the INTERGROWTH-21st team regarding your experience with piloting these charts, or to request modifications to the resources (e.g. language; different institutional logo), please contact: intergrowth@wrh.ox.ac.uk. 

For the fetal growth standards and symphysis-fundal height standards, we advise all healthcare professionals and researchers to read our gestational age guidelines.
